Receive purchases from a company of the group through the Incoming
transit of ``purchase_stock_transit_receipt``, whatever their incoterm.

The validation of the seller's delivery validates the buyer's Transit
receipt with the shipped quantities and lots: the goods are in the
buyer's stock while in transit. The usual reception of the buyer's
warehouse waits for the goods to arrive, and only its validation sets
the received quantity of the purchase.

This complements *Sync the receipt with the delivery* of
``purchase_sale_inter_company``, where the seller's delivery validates
the buyer's reception itself (dropship, or a warehouse that does not
record its receptions).

Configuration
=============

In the settings of the buyer company, under the inter-company rules,
enable *Sync picking*, then *Through the transit receipt*.

The seller company must create sale orders from purchase orders.

Known issues / Roadmap
======================

Partial deliveries depend on the receipt synchronisation of
``purchase_sale_inter_company``, unchanged by this module: the next
delivery must be synchronised with the open backorder of the receipt,
and a delivery without backorder must only cancel the rest of the
receipt (OCA/multi-company 14.0 since ``c7861ab3``).
